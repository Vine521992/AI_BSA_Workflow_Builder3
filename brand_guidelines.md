# Universal internal BA brand guidelines

Purpose: consistent internal IPOC / DXBSA Business Analyst deliverables.

## Brand identity
- Brand: Internal BA Automation Framework
- Audience: business analysts, product owners, architects, delivery teams and stakeholders
- Style: restrained, professional, accessible enterprise consulting

## Palette (starter defaults — replace with approved corporate values)
- Primary navy: `#123B5D`
- Secondary blue: `#2676A5`
- Accent teal: `#00A6A6`
- Body text: `#263238`
- Muted text: `#607D8B`
- Page background: `#FFFFFF`
- Table header background: `#EAF1F5`

These are provisional framework colours, not a claim about an employer/client's official brand. Client branding is not applied unless an approved project override explicitly requests it.

## Layout
- A4 portrait by default; landscape only for very wide matrices.
- Consistent title, document-control block, section numbering, headers/footers and page numbers.
- Use restrained colour, clear tables, sufficient contrast and readable typography.
- No decorative imagery unless supplied as an approved asset.
- Do not fetch remote fonts, logos, CSS or images.
