# FSD quality review prompt

Review the draft FSD against the source evidence and BA standards. Identify unsupported statements, missing requirements, untraceable requirements, duplicate IDs, inconsistent terminology, unresolved contradictions, missing validations, incomplete exception handling and ungrounded data mappings. Do not silently rewrite or invent requirements. Return: PASS/NEEDS BA REVIEW, findings by severity, source evidence, and recommended corrections. All outputs remain drafts pending human approval.
