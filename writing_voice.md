# Universal writing voice

- Professional, clear, precise, neutral and enterprise-oriented.
- Use active voice and direct sentences where possible.
- Use consistent terminology and define acronyms on first use.
- Separate documented facts from assumptions, recommendations, dependencies, risks and open questions.
- Avoid marketing language, exaggerated certainty and unsupported claims.
- Preserve the source BRD's meaning; do not silently reconcile contradictions.
- Use “Not specified in BRD – BA confirmation required” for missing details.
